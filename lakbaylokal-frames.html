Frame representation

LakbayLokal

The system's knowledge organized as frames: named concepts with slots, default values, constraints, inheritance and attached procedures. Every frame below maps to something the page actually stores or computes.

Frame hierarchy (is-a and part-of)

is-a links pass slots and defaults down to specializations. part-of links show how a trip is assembled.

Destination Match

is-a: Recommendation

The score the system computes for each curated destination. The top three are listed; the current one is shown first.

Procedural attachments (demons)

Small procedures that fire when a slot is needed, added or changed.

TriggerFrame.slotEffect
if-changedTraveler Profile.*sync the plan's profile, refresh the Recommended tab, update budget meters
if-changedTraveler Profile.daysadd empty days, or remove empty days from the end (refuse if the last day has stops)
if-addedDay.stopssort by time
if-neededDestination.nearbyrank nearby spots by travel time, then pick 3 to 5 with different types (city, beach, nature ...)
if-neededTrip.days (from Curated Destination)copy the day ideas, cap stops by pace, add a "Lodging (estimate)" stop each night except the last

Worked instance

One traveler, filled in.

Traveler Profile #t1 destination: Cebu days: 3 month: November party: Couple travelers: 2 daily_budget: 3,500 PHP pace: Balanced interests: { Food & markets, History, Nature & hikes } Destination Cebu (Curated) region: Visayas type: city crowd_level: 2 typical_cost_per_day: 3,000 lodging_per_night: 900 best_months: Jan to May avoid_months: Sep, Oct day_ideas: [ "Old Cebu on foot", "Canyon water and sardines", "Hills, gardens and seafood" ] Destination Match #m1 (t1, Cebu) interest_fit: +9 budget_fit: +3 season_fit: 0 (November is neither best nor avoid) reasons: ✓ Matches Food & markets, History, Nature & hikes ✓ Fits your 3,500 PHP daily budget Trip #p1 title: "Cebu in 3 days" Day 1 "Old Cebu on foot" cost: 510 + lodging 900 = 1,410 over_budget: false Day 2 "Canyon water and sardines" cost: 3,650 + lodging 900 = 4,550 over_budget: true Day 3 "Hills, gardens and seafood" cost: 1,850 (no lodging, last day) over_budget: false cost_per_person: 7,810 budget_left: 10,500 − 7,810 = 2,690

Inference trace

  1. Read the Traveler Profile slots from the form.
  2. Look up the Destination frame. If it is Curated, show its day ideas and a Destination Match. If not, fire nearby.
  3. Score every Curated Destination (interest, budget, season, quiet) and list the top three.
  4. On "Build this trip" or "Add to my plan", copy Day frames from the Destination into the Trip and fire the if-added sort.
  5. Compute Day.cost and Trip.budget_left on demand. Mark any Day that exceeds the daily budget.
  6. The traveler edits any slot by hand. The if-changed demons refresh everything and save.
