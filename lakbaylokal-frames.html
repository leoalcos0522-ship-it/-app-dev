Frame representation

LakbayLokal

The system's knowledge organized as frames: named concepts with slots, default values, constraints, inheritance and attached procedures. Every frame below maps to something the page actually stores or computes.

is-a: a frame inherits its parent's slots and defaults. part-of: the frame is a component of another.

Destination

is-a: Placespecialized by: Curated, Uncurated

A selectable place. Every destination has a name and map position. Curated destinations add the slots marked below.

SlotType / facetMeaning
namestringe.g. "Cebu, Philippines"
regionenumLuzon | Palawan | Visayas | Mindanao
positionlat, lngused to estimate travel time
island_groupstringdecides road vs boat travel
typeenumcity | beach | nature | culture | island
tags *subset of interestswhat the place is good for
typical_cost_per_day *PHP per personcompared with the traveler's daily budget
lodging_per_night *PHP per person sharing×1.6 when travelling solo
crowd_level *1 quiet · 2 · 3 busyfavors quiet places when requested
best_months *set of monthsgives a bonus
avoid_months *set of monthstyphoon or rainy season, gives a penalty
day_ideas *list of Day frames2 to 3 ready-made days of stops
nearbyif-neededwhen there are no day ideas, compute Nearby Spots within about 3 hours

* only Curated Destinations fill these slots. Uncurated destinations inherit empty values and fall back to the nearby procedure.

Nearby Spot

is-a: Place

A town, beach or nature stop used by the "Trips You Might Also Like" section. About 110 are stored, including all destinations.

SlotType / facetMeaning

mountain_roadboolean, default falseslows road travel
travel_time_from(origin)if-neededroad: distance × 1.4 ÷ 45 km/h · mountain road: × 2.2 ÷ 22 · boat: distance × 1.2 ÷ 35 + 0.75 h · only known ferry links are allowed
reasonif-neededchosen from the type pair (nature, beach, culture, city, island) plus a day-trip or overnight tail

Destination Match

is-a: Recommendation

The score the system computes for each curated destination. The top three are listed; the current one is shown first.

SlotType / facetRule
interest_fitif-needed+3 for every interest the destination's tags share with the traveler
budget_fitif-neededbudget ≥ typical cost: +3 · within 80%: +1 · below that: −8 × the shortfall
season_fitif-neededbest month: +3 · avoid month: −4
quiet_bonusif-neededif "Quiet/less-crowded places" is picked: + (4 − crowd_level)
reasonslist of strings✓ positives and ! warnings shown on the card

Procedural attachments (demons)

Small procedures that fire when a slot is needed, added or changed.

TriggerFrame.slotEffect
if-changedTraveler Profile.*sync the plan's profile, refresh the Recommended tab, update budget meters
if-changedTraveler Profile.daysadd empty days, or remove empty days from the end (refuse if the last day has stops)
if-addedDay.stopssort by time
if-neededDestination.nearbyrank nearby spots by travel time, then pick 3 to 5 with different types (city, beach, nature ...)
if-neededTrip.days (from Curated Destination)copy the day ideas, cap stops by pace, add a "Lodging (estimate)" stop each night except the last

Inference trace

StepWhat the system doesFrames and slots used
1Read the formTraveler Profile (all slots)
2Look up the chosen destination. If curated, show its day ideas. If not, find nearby spots.Destination.day_ideas · Destination.nearby
3Score every curated destination and list the top threeDestination Match (interest, budget, season, quiet)
4On "Build this trip" or "Add to my plan", copy days into the trip and sort stops by timeTrip.days · Day.stops (if-added)
5Work out each day's cost, the trip total and the budget left. Flag days over budget.Day.cost · Day.over_budget · Trip.budget_left
6The traveler edits anything. The system refreshes everything and saves.if-changed procedures · System.storage
